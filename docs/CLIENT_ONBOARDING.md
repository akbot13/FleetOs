# FleetOs client onboarding

A guide for cab operators starting with FleetOs.

## 1. What FleetOs does

FleetOs is a booking and dispatch tool for cab operators. Customers book a ride, you assign a driver, the driver runs the trip from their phone, and you track cash from trip to handover. Everything sits under your own brand.

## 2. Roles

- **Admin:** you or your office staff. Logs in with email and password. Manages bookings, drivers, pricing, dispatch and cash.
- **Driver:** logs in on their phone. Sees assigned trips, starts and completes them, and records cash.
- **Customer:** books a ride without an account. Sees the status of their booking.

## 3. First-day checklist

1. Log in as admin with the email and password we gave you.
2. Check your organization branding: brand name, and logo and color if you have them.
3. Set your pricing: city per-km rate, outstation per-km rate, airport flat fare, sightseeing per hour, night surcharge, waiting charges, and driver stay per night.
4. Add your UPI ID and upload your UPI QR code so drivers can show it to customers.
5. Optional: add your Google review link. It is included in the thank-you message after a trip.

## 4. Adding drivers and vehicles

For each driver, enter:

- Name
- Phone number (the driver uses this to log in, so it must be correct)
- Vehicle model
- Vehicle plate number

New drivers start as **pending approval**. A driver cannot take trips until you approve them.

## 5. Creating bookings

Customers can book themselves, or you can create a booking for them. Each booking has:

- **Trip type:** city, outstation, airport, or sightseeing
- **Pickup and drop** locations
- **Stops** along the way, if any
- **Scheduled time**
- **Notes** for the driver, such as luggage or a child seat

Bookings start as pending until a driver is assigned.

## 6. Dispatching a driver

Open the dispatch screen, pick a pending booking, and choose a driver. The booking is marked confirmed with the dispatch time. The driver gets the trip on their phone and confirms it. The customer is sent a confirmation with the driver and vehicle details (see known limits for delivery).

## 7. Completing a trip

1. The driver starts the trip. Their status changes to on-trip.
2. For temporary drivers the app asks for an odometer photo before the trip starts and before it ends.
3. The driver completes the trip. Their status returns to free.

## 8. Recording cash collection and handover

1. After completing a trip, the driver records the amount collected and whether it was cash or UPI.
2. When the driver gives cash to the office, they record a handover for the amount.
3. You review the handover and approve it. Approved handovers are your record that the cash reached you.

## 9. Known limits

- Driver login is invite-only. It checks the phone number against the driver list. There is no OTP yet.
- There are no online payments. Collect cash or UPI in person.
- SMS and WhatsApp messages are not live yet. Messages are logged but not delivered until a messaging provider is connected.
- Undo handover is not available. Check the amount before you approve.
